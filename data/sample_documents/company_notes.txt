Northbridge Analytics is a fictional mid-market data-analytics software
company founded in 2016 and headquartered in the fictional city of
Rivermont. The company builds embedded analytics dashboards that other
software vendors license and resell inside their own products. All figures
in this note are invented for demonstration purposes only.

For its most recently completed fiscal year, Northbridge Analytics reported
annual recurring revenue of 42 million dollars, up 28 percent year over
year. The company serves 610 active customers, most of them mid-market
software vendors in logistics, healthcare scheduling, and property
management verticals.

Headcount stands at 340 employees, with roughly 40 percent of staff in
engineering and product roles. The founding CEO, Dana Whitfield, remains
the company's primary public spokesperson and, according to internal notes,
is also the sole owner of several key enterprise-sales relationships.

Northbridge Analytics has raised a total of 65 million dollars across three
funding rounds and has not yet pursued a public offering. Management notes
describe a target of reaching 60 million dollars in annual recurring
revenue within two fiscal years, driven primarily by expansion into the
insurance-technology vertical.

Customer retention has been a recurring topic in quarterly reviews. Net
revenue retention was reported at 108 percent last year, while gross
logo churn was reported at 9 percent, concentrated among the smallest
tier of customers.
