The following market notes describe a fictional embedded-analytics software
market and are provided for demonstration purposes only; all figures are
invented and do not describe any real company or industry report.

Analysts covering the fictional embedded-analytics category estimate the
total addressable market at 8.2 billion dollars, expanding at a compound
annual growth rate of 14 percent over the next five years. Growth is
attributed to software vendors increasingly embedding analytics directly
into their products rather than asking end users to adopt a separate
business-intelligence tool.

Three competitor categories are commonly discussed in this fictional
market. The first is large horizontal business-intelligence platforms
adding embeddable components. The second is open-source charting and
dashboard toolkits, which compete primarily on cost and developer control.
The third is specialist embedded-analytics vendors, the category Northbridge
Analytics is assumed to compete in for purposes of this demonstration.

Buyer research summarized in these notes suggests that 62 percent of
enterprise software buyers now rank integration depth and time-to-embed as
a top-three purchase criterion, ahead of raw dashboard customization. This
is described as a meaningful shift from three years earlier, when pricing
was the dominant purchase criterion cited by buyers.

Pricing pressure remains a theme: average contract values in the fictional
embedded-analytics category are estimated to have declined 6 percent over
the last two years even as the overall market has grown, consistent with
increased competition from open-source alternatives.
